---
title: "Le Rouge et le Noir - Stendhal"
description: "Stendhal's novel about social ambition in the post Napoleonic era"
---

Le Rouge et le Noir by Stendhal is such a fascinating read. Beyond the crazy storyline, the intense characters, and all the psychological drama, I really like being immersed in the 19th century. It makes me think of how france, where I grew up, was a few centuries ago and I can definitely see its influence on how modern French society has evolved. Also, the story happens right after the Napoleonic wars, and the author makes subtle references to them, which feels like a nice easter egg when reading. In short, definitely read this book, but make sure to have enough time because it is a long book!
